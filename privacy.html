Privacy Policy – YT Report

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Who we are

YT Report is an internal reporting tool operated by Pham Viet Phuong. Contact: vietphuong94@gmail.com.

2. Data we access

Only after a YouTube channel owner signs in with Google and grants consent, YT Report requests these read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly – channel name, subscriber count, list of videos and their public statistics;
  • yt-analytics.readonly – YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, impressions, click-through rate, traffic by country);
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly – estimated revenue reports.

We do not access email, contacts, files, passwords or any other Google account data. We cannot upload, modify or delete any content.

3. How we use the data

The data is used solely to produce internal performance reports and dashboards for the channels that authorized the tool, and to support content and operations decisions for those channels. We do not use the data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

4. Storage and security

Data and access tokens are stored on a computer controlled by the operator, protected by disk encryption and account login. Reports may be shared internally (for example, via a Google Sheet visible only to authorized team members). Access tokens are never shared with third parties.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer YouTube user data to third parties, except where required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Historical statistics are kept for as long as the channel remains connected, to show performance trends. When a channel owner revokes access or asks us to remove the channel, we delete its access token and, on request, its stored statistics within 30 days.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke YT Report's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

YT Report's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YT Report uses YouTube API Services; by authorizing it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

9. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
